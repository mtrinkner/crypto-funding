# Forensic audit, 2026-10-29

Scope: every study, the registry, the forward test (xv_v1) and its ledgers. The
code was read and the key numbers were re-run against the repo's own functions.
Nothing in the repo was edited apart from this file. Every number below was
reproduced in the pinned env `/opt/anaconda3/envs/edge-audit`. The base
anaconda Python segfaults in pyarrow whenever it reads parquet.

**Bottom line.** No signal look-ahead in any trading rule. But four errors flatter
printed results, and one of them is live: the forward test is holding dYdX
markets that are in final settlement. No verdict flips from fail to pass. Two
printed positives are gone: the strategy 20/21 full-sample carry numbers, and
the "crypto momentum pays only when it's volatile" result.

## Severity 1: changes a published number or the live test

### A1. Single-venue carry never charges liquidations (strategies 20, 21, 22)
`pit_rerun.py:61-80`, `carry_switch.py:51-81` and `funding_carry.py:125` compute
`pos*(carry - rf) - switches*cost`. The 3x short perp is never stopped out.
`funding_carry.py:170` only *counts* rallies, using closes and a forward
`shift(-3)`, and the count never touches P&L. `validate.py` does charge
liquidations (on the day's high), but only on the holdout. The full-sample
numbers carried into the registry don't.

Re-run with validate.py's own trigger (high / previous close − 1 ≥ 1/3 − 3%,
5% penalty plus re-entry legs):

| Book | Liquidations | Excess | Monthly Sharpe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20, always on | 147 / 24,839 held coin-days | 3.0% → **−5.6%** | 0.51 → **−1.08** |
| 21, 30-day switch | 92 / 15,983 | 7.6% → **2.2%** | 1.92 → **0.72** |

A trigger that looks only at closes would find 45 and 28 of these events, so it
understates liquidations by about 3x. **Action:** add an annotation to the
inherited entries for 20 and 21. The "point-in-time: 3.0%, Sharpe 0.51" and
"1.92" figures are pre-liquidation and must say so wherever they are quoted.

### A2. Dead dYdX markets treated as tradable (strategy 25 and live xv_v1)
After final settlement, the dYdX indexer keeps printing `rate = 0`, so the
spread becomes pure Hyperliquid funding. Examples: FET from 2024-06, MATIC
2024-09, FTM 2025-01, MKR 2025-09. `ingest_dydx.py:101-109` and
`cross_venue.py:41-48` don't filter on market status.
- **Backtest:** 46 of 14,112 held coin-days, but 4.8% of the gross spread.
  Dropping them takes excess from 1.06% to **0.61%** and Sharpe from 0.39 to
  **0.24**.
- **Live:** `forward_xv.py:215,237` builds `listed` from `/perpetualMarkets`
  without checking `status`. Today (2026-10-29), 5 of the 22 coins xv_v1 has
  ever held are in `FINAL_SETTLEMENT`: ENA, ONDO, ZRO, LIT and XPL. ENA, ONDO
  and ZRO have nonzero exposure in the latest position set. A settled market
  can't be traded, so those legs earn a fake spread.
- `forward_xv.py` is hash-frozen, so it can't be patched. **Action:** record a
  registry note now, before more weeks are scored. The 52-week verdict must be
  reported twice: as frozen, and excluding legs on non-ACTIVE markets. Any fix
  is xv_v2, a new trial.

### A3. The volatility-tercile table is look-ahead (strategy 28 README)
README:108-114 ("volatile third, +110%, Sharpe 1.41") is reproduced exactly by
`pd.qcut(trailing_vol, 3)` over the **full sample**. The script was never
committed; it exists only in commit e0d4284's message. With point-in-time
terciles (each day ranked against its own expanding history, at least 180
days):

| Tercile | Full-sample cutoffs | Point-in-time |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | −0.09 | −0.12 |
| Middle | −0.10 | **0.51** |
| Volatile | **1.41** | 0.47 |

The finding doesn't survive. In addition, 10 days carry 141% of the book's
total P&L. **Action:** retract the table and the "trade momentum only when
volatile, test forward" idea it motivated.

### A4. Luck bar and DSR use weekly units with monthly observations
`deflated_sharpe.py:71,79` defaults to `periods_per_year = 252/5`. Every caller
passes a *month* count as `n_obs`: `validate.py:210,230,286`,
`daily_momentum.py:108,115`, `vol_momentum.py:75,87`, `out_of_time.py:124`.
That inflates the bar by sqrt(50.4/12) = 2.05x. For strategy 27 the bar is
printed as 3.23 against a correct 1.58, and the DSR as 0.008 against 0.028.
The error makes the tests stricter, so it flips nothing. But every bar in the
README is about 2x too high. Separately, `registry.py status` hardcodes the
equity default of 348 weekly observations (6.7 years) and prints a bar of
1.108. For the roughly 3.4-year Hyperliquid samples that bar is too *low*
(about 1.6). **Action:** pass `periods_per_year=12` wherever months are passed,
and pass the real sample length to `status`.

## Severity 2: integrity of the trial count

### B1. The inherited trial count is stale
The `inherit` entry froze edge-audit at **304** trials. Edge-audit now counts
**856**, so N here is 317 against roughly 869 under the repo's own principle
that "moving house does not reset the luck bar". At about 3.4 years of monthly
data, the bar moves from about 1.58 to about 1.75. **Action:** decide a policy:
either a periodic `inherit` top-up entry (append-only, so this is allowed), or
a documented decision that the search spaces are now disjoint. Today it is
neither.

### B2. Both cross-venue legs are risk-modeled off Hyperliquid prices
`cross_venue.py:55-56,79` and `forward_xv.py:253-261` liquidate the dYdX long on
Hyperliquid's low, and mark basis from Hyperliquid's premium only. dYdX books
are far thinner (35bp half-spread, measured), so their wicks are larger. The
size is unquantified because there is no dYdX OHLC in `data/`. The direction
is upward bias.

## Severity 3: data hygiene
- **OKX candles on Hong Kong time.** `ingest_okx.py:97` requests `bar=1D`, which
  closes at UTC+8. It should be `1Dutc`. The unconfirmed last bar is also kept.
  This affects strategies 20-22 only.
- **OKX universe chosen by today's volume.** `ingest_okx.py:59-66` selects on
  current 24h volume. This survivorship is already known for 20-22, but it
  isn't flagged in the code.
- **dYdX survivorship.** `ingest_dydx.py:106` matches against today's market
  list, so 15 of the 110 universe coins have no match (FTT, RNDR→RENDER,
  FARTCOIN, …). In the live position set, `kPEPE` doesn't map to dYdX's naming
  either.
- **Smaller issues.** 25 BitMEX coin-days have only 1-2 of their 3 funding
  prints. T-bills are taken at the same day's DTB3 print (about 1 day early).
  A coin delisted while held never has its exit charged in strategy 27. The
  last Hyperliquid candle (2026-10-09) is partial. All negligible.

## Checked and correct
- **No signal look-ahead.** Signals use `shift(1)`. The top-20 universe uses
  trailing 30-day volume through yesterday and includes delisted Hyperliquid
  coins.
- **Funding.** Units are hourly for both venues. Signs are correct for
  short-perp and cross-venue legs. Hyperliquid's 8h → 1h switch on 2023-06-08
  is handled.
- **BitMEX.** The inverse XBTUSD contract is correct under daily rebalancing.
  The ETHUSD quanto cross-term −r_e·r_b is correct, and the record of the
  original precommitment error is intact.
- **Costs and statistics.** Costs are charged on both legs at entry, exit,
  universe changes and the final unwind. Monthly Sharpe uses non-overlapping
  sums. PSR includes skew and kurtosis. The stationary bootstrap is valid.
- **Data.** No duplicates and no forward-fill gaps in the Hyperliquid or BitMEX
  panels. The 2020-03-12 moves are real.
- **Ledgers.** The registry has 12 entries and the forward ledger 5, and both
  hash chains verify. `forward_xv.py` matches its frozen SHA-256 (`179cf304…`).

## Where this leaves the research
Nothing here was ever close to the 1.5 target. After this audit, the only live
question, xv_v1, is compromised in two ways. It was already known to have no
capacity: dYdX is about 35bp wide with under $0.05m a day on most held coins.
It now also books spread on settled markets. My recommendation is to let it
run to week 52 as a record, scored both ways, and not to spend more research
time on funding dispersion. A prop desk would close this book: the gross
dispersion only exists in markets that can't absorb size.
